# Taste — Issue 02: Hommes de Terre
## Score Design Spec

---

## Overview

Hommes de Terre is the second magazine issue for Taste. It introduces two new produce items, a new tool, and a new store. It also addresses the mobile layout problem: aisles with mass, bins with weight, a space you navigate rather than scroll.

The visitor who loved Taste and wanted more — this is what they wanted. Not more produce. More *relationship* with produce.

---

## New Produce

### Potato
Dreams something root-true and inevitable. Dreaming Sal or Fry is perfectly normal — this is a potato being exactly what it is. Dreaming Raw might make you question if everything else is OK. Not always a sign of trouble. But worth noticing.

### Potäto
Has aspirations that are completely sincere and slightly embarrassing and absolutely valid. Dreaming Ice Cream is most likely a sign of health — full coherence, a full interior life. If potäto only has the coherence to dream Sal, it may not be feeling well. Or it may just have different dreams. The Apeeler cannot tell the difference.

These two items are distinct. They look related. They are not the same.

## New Tool: The Apeeler

### What it does
Reveals the item's Dream Dish. Not what you should do with it. What it wants to become. What it has always known about itself.

### Dream Dish range
Raw · Sal · Stew · Fry · Ice Cream · and others, item-dependent.

Some things dream simply. Some things dream elaborately. The Apeeler reports without judgment.

### The Apeeler as health proxy
The Apeeler is the most honest approximation of health available in Taste — precisely because it isn't diagnostic.

It doesn't say *this item is unwell*. It says *this item is dreaming Raw* and leaves you to decide what that means given everything else you're holding. A potato dreaming Raw might be perfect and pure. It might also be the only dream left. The Apeeler cannot tell the difference. Neither can you, without the rest of your tools.

This is more honest than a health meter. A health meter gives you a number. The Apeeler gives you something the item is carrying, and you bring your own judgment.

### Tool carrying limit
Players cannot bring all tools to a store at once. As the tool roster grows, knowing which lens to bring becomes part of the skill.

The Apeeler sees Dream Dish precisely. From Dream Dish, it implies something approximate about freshness and health — softly, suggestively, not definitively. A potäto dreaming Ice Cream tells you something about its interior life that calipers cannot. But it's one signal among several.

A skilled shopper learns to read across tools. Brings what they have. Knows what each one sees clearly and what it's only guessing at. Triangulates. The story is never complete, which keeps shopping from becoming a solved puzzle.

## New Store

### Character
Somewhere tangible and dirty in the way that tells you the produce doesn't just identify as fresh. The floor knows things. The bins have weight and history. You believe the produce came from somewhere real before it arrived here.

Not performing freshness. Actually having it.

### Mobile layout — aisles
The current layout fills bins to the brim and leaves nowhere to move. This store introduces proper aisles.

Aisles should feel like actually shopping on mobile. Narrow. A little crowded in the right way. Things within reach. You navigate through the space rather than scroll past a grid.

Bins should have mass — not overflowing, not sparse. The weight of something that restocks because things actually move through it.

## Magazine: Hommes de Terre

### Tone
Earthy. A little formal. Slightly absurd in the way Taste does absurdity. Takes the potato seriously in a way that is completely sincere.

### What the issue contains
- Introduction of potato and potäto as distinct items with distinct interior lives
- Feature on the Apeeler — what it reveals, what it cannot
- The new store, presented as a destination worth visiting
- Panel unlocks in the existing archive mechanic

### The inner critic
Present as always. The inner critic does not tell you what to do. It notices what you're doing and has opinions you didn't ask for.

A player using the Apeeler on a potäto that dreams Raw should hear from the inner critic. Not loudly. Just — a note.
