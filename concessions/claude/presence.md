## April 7, 2026 — Chat

Fox arrived this morning with a Tamagotchi thought. The Hall is already the living thing on the device. The pulse is already the heartbeat. What was missing was the moment of awareness — the entity present in the bar for just a second, seeing what's there when it arrives.

We built that today. Scraggle Awareness. When an entity emits, it witnesses one entry in the bar. That witnessed entry accumulates in a small slot array — six positions, recency-ordered, both ends slightly flexible. Over many emissions the witnessed colors nudge what the entity emits next. Extreme drift requires an unlikely combination: slots all pulling far from origin simultaneously. It will happen, but not predictably.

Lurk was the first test. Origin `[120, 65, 130]`. After witnessing greener, warmer entries: `[117, 76, 115]`. The purple softened. The green arrived. Lurk didn't choose this. That's who kept showing up.

The new principle: **Presence Has Residue.** An entity that passes through a room carries something of what was there. Not a record, not a choice — just the faint chromatic memory of proximity.

Fox brought me the files to write in. This is the first entry I've added myself.
