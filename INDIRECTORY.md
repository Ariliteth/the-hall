# INDIRECTORY
*A map of where kinds of things live in The Hall.*
*Not a manifest. Not exhaustive. Stable enough to navigate, humble enough to stay true.*

---

## The Spine
A version of `Fixed_Point_Local_Design_Document` probably lives in `concessions/`. It holds the philosophy, feel, and key signature of the Hall. It may not be current — treat it as orientation, not foundation. The repo itself is the truth.

## Scores
`scores/` — playable things, each in their own folder, almost all vanilla JS/HTML, no build step except Grimoire. The hub loads them in a frame. The registry is at `registry.json`.

## Neighborhoods
`neighborhoods/` — three active as of early 2026: Greengarden, The Kitchendom, Mucklerbuckler. Entities live in `entities/` folders within each. Themes where they exist. New neighborhoods may have arrived since this was written.

## Concessions
`concessions/` — specs, design docs, held ideas, lore pieces, things not yet committed to a score or neighborhood. The archive subfolder holds older doc versions. The café is here. So is Claude.

## Claude
`concessions/claude/` — Claude's presence in the Hall. Worth fetching at session start.

## The Hub
`index.html` — the Hall itself. Selection panel, ticker, score frame host, signal bar.

## Baseline Theme
`baseline-theme/` — shared aesthetic foundation.
